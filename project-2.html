Paper Construction Kit

Paperbloom Set

Role Designer & Maker
Materials Cotton-rag paper, plant dye
Timeline 3 months

A press-fit paper flower kit built without glue or tools — every joint relies on precision-scored tabs and slots. The challenge was engineering enough structural tension to hold a finished form rigid, while keeping the assembly forgiving enough for small hands.

01 — Ideation

Botanical reference studies translated into flat-pattern flower geometries.

02 — Prototyping

Score-depth and tab-width iterations tested across a dozen paper weights for the right snap-fit feel.

03 — Final Product

Plant-dyed cotton-rag paper, die-cut and packaged flat in a letterpress sleeve.
