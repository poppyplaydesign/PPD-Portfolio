Science in a Bucket

Ooze Labs Big Buckets

Role Product Manager & Designer
Materials Chemicals and various compounds
Timeline 8 months

With so many T&K crystal and slime products launched over the years, this one was a good challenge in differentiation and value addition. We envisioned these as a series of eye-catching buckets falling under our chemistry-focused sub brand Ooze Labs. While the challenge with the crystal kit was to thoroughly test and improve the materials and experiments to make it easy enough to get beautiful and substantial crystals, the challenge with the slime kit was to design experiments that encouraged scientific inquiry around this material that is usually only seen as a medium for fun crafts. The result is a series of value-packed science experiment kits with quality materials, instructional and educational content and a neat storage solution in the form of a fun bucket.
